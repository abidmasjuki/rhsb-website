Types of Hearing Loss

Understanding hearing decibel levels and sound perception

26 - 40 dB

Mild Hearing Loss

Difficulty hearing soft sounds like dripping water, rustling leaves, or quiet whispers.

Schedule Assessment
41 - 55 dB

Moderate Hearing Loss

Difficulty understanding conversation or soft musical tones over room background noise.

Schedule Assessment
71 - 90 dB

Severe Hearing Loss

Speech is inaudible without amplification. Only loud shouting or crying sounds can be heard.

Schedule Assessment
91+ dB

Profound Hearing Loss

Extreme hearing difficulty. Only extremely loud industrial sounds like jackhammers are felt.

What is RPWP ?

RPWP is an acronym for Rumah Pengasih Warga Prihatin (Home for Love & Compassionate Community). Established in 2005 by 20 co-founders, RPWP is a stand-alone social enterprise that sustains a 260 pax sustainable community comprising all types of charity members, staff, students, and co-founders without depending on external alms.

In early 2020 during the emergency COVID-19 outbreak, RPWP converted school halls into a face shield and PPE factory for the Ministry of Health, giving birth to RPWP Healthcare Sdn Bhd.
