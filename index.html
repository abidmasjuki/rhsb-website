What is RPWP?

RPWP is an acronym for Rumah Pengasih Warga Prihatin (Home for Love & Compassionate Community). Established in 2005 by 20 co-founders, RPWP is a stand-alone social enterprise that sustains a 260 pax sustainable community comprising all types of charity members, staff, students, and co-founders without depending on external donations.

In early 2020 during the emergency COVID-19 outbreak, RPWP converted school halls into a face shield and PPE factory for the Ministry of Health, giving birth to RPWP Healthcare Sdn Bhd.
