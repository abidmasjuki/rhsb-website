Enhanced External Counterpulsation (EECP)

Non-Invasive Outpatient Cardiovascular Therapy

Clinically proven non-pharmacological outpatient treatment designed to augment coronary diastolic perfusion pressure, increase Wall Shear Stress (WSS), promote Nitric Oxide (NO) release, and stimulate VEGF-mediated angiogenesis.

Mechanism of Action & Clinical Benefits

How EECP cuffs synchronized with ECG cardiac cycles enhance microvascular circulation

🫀

Diastolic Perfusion Augmentation

Inflatable cuffs wrapped around calves, thighs, and buttocks inflate sequentially during cardiac diastole, driving arterial blood backward into the coronary arteries to boost oxygen delivery to ischemic myocardium.

⚡

Systolic Unloading & LVEF Support

Cuffs deflate instantly right before systole (heartbeat), creating a vacuum effect in peripheral arteries. This significantly reduces left ventricular workload and cardiac oxygen demand.

🌿

WSS, Nitric Oxide & Angiogenesis

Increased endothelial Wall Shear Stress (WSS) triggers endothelin-1 suppression and Nitric Oxide (NO) synthesis, promoting VEGF angiogenesis and natural coronary collateral vessel formation.

35-Hour Treatment Protocol

Standard clinical EECP regimen consists of 35 one-hour daily sessions administered over 7 consecutive weeks. Patients relax comfortably on a treatment bed while ECG electrodes synchronize cuff pressure inflation with their resting heartbeat.

EECP therapy is recommended for patients experiencing chronic stable angina, coronary artery disease, fatigue, or post-cardiac rehabilitation under medical supervision.
