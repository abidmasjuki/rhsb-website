🫀 Cardiovascular Innovation

OMAY Enhanced External Counterpulsation (EECP)

Manufactured by OMAY (Guangzhou) Medical Technologies under Technical Director Dr. Zhou Shao Chun and pioneer inventor Dr. Zheng Zhen Shen.

How OMAY EECP Works

EECP is a non-surgical, outpatient therapy. Three sets of pneumatic cuffs wrap around your calves, lower thighs, and upper thighs. Synchronized with your ECG, cuffs inflate during heart relaxation (diastole) to pump blood into coronary arteries, and deflate rapidly during heart contraction (systole).

🌊

Wall Shear Stress

🧪

Nitric Oxide (NO) Boost

🌱

Promotes natural collateral blood vessel growth ("Natural Bypass").

🌱 Social Enterprise

Rumah Pengasih Warga Prihatin

RPWP (Rumah Pengasih Warga Prihatin) was founded in 2005 by 20 co-founders as a self-sustaining social enterprise. Today, RPWP sustains a 260 pax sustainable community comprising charity members, staff, students, and co-founders without depending on external donations.

During COVID-19 in 2020, RPWP converted school halls to manufacture face shields and PPE for the Ministry of Health, establishing RPWP Healthcare Sdn Bhd.
