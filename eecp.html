What is EECP Therapy?

Understanding cardiac diastole augmentation and arterial pressure synchronization

Enhanced External Counterpulsation (EECP) is a non-invasive medical procedure utilized in cardiac rehabilitation and outpatient vascular care. The treatment uses a set of pneumatic cuffs wrapped around the patient's calves, lower thighs, and upper thighs.

These cuffs inflate sequentially from calves upward during heart relaxation (diastole), pushing arterial blood backward into the coronary arteries to increase perfusion pressure. Immediately before the heart beats (systole), the cuffs deflate instantaneously, lowering vascular resistance and allowing the heart to pump blood with significantly reduced workload.

🌊

Wall Shear Stress (WSS)

Sequential compression increases blood flow velocity, generating fluid shear stress along arterial vessel walls to stimulate endothelial mechanosensors.

🧪

Nitric Oxide (NO) Boost

Stimulates endothelial Nitric Oxide release, relaxing vascular smooth muscle, restoring arterial elasticity, and reducing systemic vascular resistance.

🌱

VEGF Angiogenesis

Promotes Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor for natural collateral vessel formation, creating small micro-vascular pathways around narrowed arteries.

🫀

LVEF Augmentation

Rapid cuff deflation during heart contraction reduces cardiac afterload workload, enhancing Left Ventricular Ejection Fraction (LVEF) efficiency.

Who Can Benefit From EECP?

EECP therapy is suitable for individuals seeking non-surgical cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular wellness enhancement, or microvascular circulation support under clinical supervision:

  • ✓ Patients with chronic stable angina pectoris
  • ✓ Individuals seeking non-invasive cardiovascular circulation enhancement
  • ✓ Post-cardiac intervention rehabilitation support
  • ✓ Patients experiencing poor peripheral circulation or vascular fatigue
