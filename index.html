Audiometric Thresholds

Degrees of Hearing Loss & Perception

Understanding decibel sound levels and how hearing impairment affects daily life

26 - 40 dB

Mild Hearing Loss

Difficulty hearing soft sounds like dripping water, rustling leaves, or quiet whispers in quiet environments.

41 - 60 dB

Moderate Hearing Loss

Difficulty understanding normal conversation or soft musical tones over ambient room background noise.

61 - 85 dB

Severe Hearing Loss

Speech is inaudible without amplification. Only loud shouting, heavy traffic engines, or alarms are heard.

86+ dB

Profound Hearing Loss

Extreme hearing difficulty. Only extremely loud industrial sounds like jackhammers or jet engines are felt.

Unsure where your hearing perception falls? Our certified clinical audiologists conduct comprehensive Pure Tone Audiometry (PTA) in diagnostic acoustic suites.

The RPWP Healthcare Genesis Story

How 130 Kilometers of Medical Fabric Birthed RPWP Healthcare

During the 2020 COVID-19 frontline crisis, national emergency PPE shipments were intercepted overseas, leaving Malaysian hospital frontliners wearing plastic garbage bags. The RPWP community mobilized, transforming their community halls into emergency factories that produced over 130 km of protective PPE fabric into protective isolation gowns and face shields. This movement birthed RPWP Healthcare Sdn Bhd.

What is RPWP ?

RPWP is an acronym for Rumah Pengasih Warga Prihatin (Home for Love & Compassionate Community). Established in 2005 by 20 co-founders, RPWP is a stand-alone social enterprise that sustains the RPWP sustainable community comprising charity members, staff, students, and co-founders without depending on external alms.

In early 2020 during the emergency COVID-19 outbreak, RPWP converted school halls into a face shield and PPE factory for the Ministry of Health, giving birth to RPWP Healthcare Sdn Bhd.
