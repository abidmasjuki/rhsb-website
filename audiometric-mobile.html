Legal Compliance & Regulations

Compliance is Not Optional

Responsibilities to ensure safety & health at the workplace lie with those who create the risk

📜

OSH Act 1994 (Amendments 2022)

Statutory duty under Act 514 requiring employers to establish hearing conservation programs and conduct annual employee noise monitoring.

🎧

OSH (Noise Exposure) Regulations 2019

Mandatory baseline and annual audiometric testing for workers exposed to noise action levels above 82 dB(A) or peak 135 dB(C).

⚖️

Non-Compliance Penalties

Failure to comply carries statutory fines up to RM 10,000 or imprisonment up to 1 year, or both per violation under DOSH guidelines.
